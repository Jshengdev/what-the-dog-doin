# JUDGES: who is grading, what they build, what they reward

Research date 2026-09-12. Deep-research report produced by a background agent from live web sources; anything marked **[inference]** is the agent's reasoning, not a found fact. Read §0 and §10 before building. The rest is reference.

## What this means for what-the-dog-doin

- **It is effectively Arga Labs' hackathon.** The registration form is owned by Arga, the title mirrors Arga's YC launch, and Arga published ArgaBench eight days before the event. ArgaBench grades agents **pass / fail / unsafe** from trusted before-and-after state across systems, with a prohibited-side-effects list. That is the rubric in the judges' heads for the 25%.
- **Every judge company sells "prove the agent did the job."** Lemma finds silent failures in traces, Arga grades from state, Userlens gates sends behind approval, Clera validates tool calls and traces them. Receipts from device state read-back (light on or off, dog stood up, frame captured) are exactly the shape they reward.
- **Robotics and smart home:** personal resonance with both Lemma hosts (an Arduino robot article, autonomous-driving research) and with Comma Capital (explicit physical-AI thesis, Robotics NYC meetups). Zero resonance with any judge's product. Originality goes up. Reliability risk goes up, because a physical device is harder to grade and harder to demo reproducibly over a virtual call. The agent's verdict: treat the device as a tool with a state contract (read, act, re-read, assert) and let the trace be what they trust, not the robot.
- **Lemma instrumentation is cheap and noticed.** One root trace per run, tools and generations as children. Hai Ta (a judge) set it up in under ten minutes and it caught a bug that had been in production for twenty days. The hosts built it.
- **A human approval gate before any write to real people.** The three ArgaBench tasks with zero passes were the ones where agents skipped the draft-for-approval step. Userlens Lumi requires approval before every send. Clera's line is "humans decide."
