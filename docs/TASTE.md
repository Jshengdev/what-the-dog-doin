## 1. The canon

[`taste-library`](../../taste-library/README.md) establishes the physics: a warm paper ground where "cards held by white light instead of borders, not a stroke over 1px anywhere", one ink at six alphas doing all text and lines, exactly one accent on the one live thing, in-between font weights, a mono for everything the system says about itself, one easing curve, and a hard whimsy budget ([README.md](../../taste-library/README.md), the identity paragraph). The values live once in [`tokens.css`](../../taste-library/tokens.css): grounds ladder, ink ladder, `--live`, status, type, radii, spacing, `--ease-signature`, the recipe classes `.ring-card .ring-panel .ring-float .ring-hover .board-recess .node-chip[data-live] .key-bevel .pill-emboss .dot-glow .input-well .btn-crunch .crunch-dark .bar-track .bar-fill .dotted-canvas .divider-dashed`, and the reduced-motion guard. Keyframes: [02-MOTION.md](../../taste-library/02-MOTION.md). Shells (PANEL, CHIP, ROW card, SEGMENTED CONTROL) and the step rail: [03-COMPONENTS.md](../../taste-library/03-COMPONENTS.md). The three-act flow and dwell mechanics: [04-FLOW.md](../../taste-library/04-FLOW.md). Use the default `:root` skin (paper-light). Do not activate the `brand-clean-blue` block ([05-NEW-BRAND.md](../../taste-library/05-NEW-BRAND.md) is the other skin; not this repo).

The laws, verbatim from [00-PRINCIPLES.md](../../taste-library/00-PRINCIPLES.md):
- Law 1 "Depth belongs to light, never to borders." Law 2 "One ink at an alpha ladder does ALL text and line work." Law 3 "Exactly one accent, reserved for the single live/active thing." ("if everything is colored, nothing is alive.")
- Law 4 "In-between font weights; never the loud stops." Law 5 "Thin-line ≤1px law." Law 6 "Hover lifts in light, never in size." Law 7 "Earn-white."
- Law 8 "The mono is the system's voice." Law 9 "Motion depicts something true." Law 10 "≤1 whimsy per surface, and never on money/gate moments." Law 11 "The page IS the onboarding; primitives repeat until fluent." ("If it needs a tooltip, it is not a primitive.")
- Companion: "Show less words always". The two modes: "greys are for working, contrast is for declaring."

The bans, verbatim (automatic fail if present): "No visible borders anywhere; any border > 1px is banned." "Pure black text". "Pure white as the default ground". "Dark mode by default". "More than one accent." "More than two font families in-frame". "UI text above ~13px (the one big stat numeral is the exception)." "Hover that scales/zooms." "Bounce / elastic / spring-overshoot easing." "Motion that depicts nothing." "UI motion > 700ms (the one ~2600ms earned stat beat is the sole exception)." "Confetti on anything but a human's yes". "Spinners for instant data (use blur-up swap). ✗ Skeleton screens (use ghost cards)." Mid-scale 20 to 40px padding inside components, "the single biggest tell of generic UI." "Decorative gradients." "a shape with no datum behind it is decoration and gets cut".

## 3. The system for this repo

World metaphor: a till receipt. The page is a paper ledger that prints one row per real step; the row being printed is the only live thing. This decides the shapes (rows, chips, dots, one key, at most one stamp) and refuses node graphs, wires, 3D, chat transcripts, dashboards.

Physics = the canon's default `:root` (paper-light, ring-shadow depth, `--live` accent). From runningpod and sia take the composition rules (monochrome except the one live thing, mono = measured truth, fail loud, rows assemble live, no chrome), not the print shadow; the ring recipes are already in `tokens.css` and cost zero lines. From sayhello take the run engine and every honesty label. From dot take the narrator line and the breathing dot. From canary and lemma take the log-row grid and the eyebrow. Why it works for two minutes: a judge sees a trigger, three things landing one at a time in the same shape, one result, and a ledger that was visibly being written the whole time. Nothing needs explaining because every row means the same thing it meant the first time (Law 11).

### 3.1 Page shell (Act 1 into Act 2)
- One route. `body` on `--page`. Container `max-w-[1100px]`, `min-h-screen`. Section air ≥64px; component interiors 2 to 14px.
- Top: wordmark (Onest, size not weight); one `text-[13px] text-ink-50` line naming the outcome ("Sell the outcome; you just click to start", `references/cofounder-onboarding-flow/README.md` line 154); the trigger bar `div.ring-card.flex.items-stretch.gap-2.rounded-2xl.bg-raised.p-2` holding `input.rounded-xl.bg-white.px-4.py-3` + `button.btn-crunch.crunch-dark.bg-ink.text-page.font-mono.text-[12px]` labeled lowercase with an arrow (`run →`). Under it, the connection strip (§3.3).
- Five-second test (runningpod): at 0:00 the judge sees the trigger, three app chips, and an empty ledger panel with eyebrow `receipts · 0 rows`. Nothing else.
- On run: `grid gap-4 lg:grid-cols-[1.7fr_1fr]`. Left = step rail over the focused stage. Right = the ledger, the persistent companion. Hover lifts in light (`.ring-hover`); the only size change is the key's 1px press.

### 3.2 Step rail and stage (the run engine)
- `STEPS = [auth, A, B, C, result]` with the app names as labels. `DWELL` ≥ 2500ms per app step so each lands (sayhello used 3400 to 7200). `furthest` derives only from ledger rows that returned success (CLAUDE.md §2: "No step is ever marked done unless the external call returned success"). `shown` walks toward it; a human gate always un-pins.
- Rail: `nav.ring-panel.rounded-2xl.bg-raised.px-4.py-3` > `ol.flex.flex-wrap` of pills, `→` separators. `data-state`: `active` = `pill-emboss bg-white text-ink` + `dot-glow text-live dash-pulse`; `done` = `text-good` dot; `todo` = 25% dot; `skipped` = `line-through`; failed = forced `text-bad`. Pill = dot + 23px app tile (`.ring-card rounded-[6px] bg-white`, the one place a brand glyph may be colored) + mono label + count chip (`3 threads`).
- Stage: `section key={shownId} class="stage-enter ring-panel min-h-[320px] rounded-2xl bg-raised p-5"`. Header row: mono eyebrow left (`step 2 · slack · chat.postMessage`), chip right (`240ms` or `FAILED`). Body: the real payload of that step as an artifact (C10 `HeaderRow` key-value rows on `112px 1fr`, bottom fade), never a paragraph about it.
- The active stage is the one hero; everything else is 9 to 11px supporting cast.

### 3.3 App-connection status
- Connection strip under the trigger: three CHIPs (`pill-emboss rounded-full bg-page px-2 py-[2px] font-mono text-[9px]`), each `[dot-glow] gmail · connected`. States: `checking…` (dot `text-ocean dash-pulse`), `connected` (`text-good`), `401 · reconnect` (`text-bad`). The check is a real auth ping on load and is row 0 in the ledger (`auth check · 3/3`). Status colors are meaning; `--live` is never used here.
- Corner mode-light (D6): fixed bottom-left, mono 10px, `live` (ocean glow) or `demo_cache` (dim). Any step served by a `// DEMO_CACHE:` also carries the warn chip `◧ cached` on its ledger row (sayhello's `cached · degraded-safe`). Never hide it; the judge's "is this real?" is answered on screen.

### 3.4 The ledger (the receipt)
- PANEL shell: `section.ring-panel.rounded-2xl.bg-raised.p-5`; header `items-baseline justify-between`: eyebrow `receipts · ledger.jsonl` left, chip `3/3 ✓` right (`dash-tick` 0.3s when it increments). Under the header, one mono sentence assembled from the file, runningpod-style: `3 steps · 3 apps · 0 failed · 1.8s total · live`. Rows are rendered from the JSONL, not from UI state; the brief's numbers regenerate from the same file.
- Row = a flat grid, not a card: `grid-cols-[28px_14px_1fr_64px_72px]` (`#01` mono numeral · state dot · `gmail · threads.list` mono 10.5px over a result line in Onest 12px `3 threads read` · `240ms` mono tabular · `14:02:11` mono), `--ink-6` hairline separators, `py-2`. Enters with `msg-in` 350ms as the call returns, never batch-rendered: the ledger visibly assembles.
- Exactly one live row: the executing row lifts to `.ring-card bg-white` (earned white) with `.node-shimmer::after` (`shimmer-sweep` 1.5s) and a live mono timer `0m 04s` (C4). When its call returns it resolves in place to a faint mono chip (`sent ✓`, `read ✓`) and drops back to flat.
- Failed row: the `FailedBadge` recipe inline, `FAILED · slack · 401 invalid_auth`, `role="alert"`, red 7% wash + 1px red ring, `dash-pop`. Skipped row: `line-through` + chip `skipped`. Cached row: `◧ cached` warn chip. Never blank, never removed.
- Click a row: it expands in place to a `board-recess` well holding the raw request and response JSON in mono 10.5px (evidence at zero distance). The stage on the left jumps to that step (`pinned`).

### 3.5 In-progress state (the narrator)
- Under the active rail pill: one mono 11px lowercase line built from the live ledger event: `reading inbox · gmail · 3 threads`, `posting to #ops · slack`, `connecting to notion…`. Present-progressive verb, app name, one count. No adjectives, no canned queue, no TTS. If nothing honest exists yet, show the timer only.
- What moves: the live dot (`dash-pulse` 1.1s), the shimmer on the live row, the timer digits. Everything else is still. No spinner, no skeleton, no progress bar (steps are discrete; the rail is the progress).
- Boot state before step 1: `connecting to gmail…` beside the dot. Empty ledger: `nothing yet · rows land as each call returns`.

### 3.6 Result reveal (Act 3)
- When the last row resolves, the stage swaps (`key` change, `blur-up` 420ms) to the result artifact: the real object the agent produced (message, record, file) as a `.ring-card bg-white` card with `HeaderRow` rows and a bottom fade. The ledger chip reads `3/3 ✓`.
- One earned numeral, once: `text-[40px] font-[340] font-numeral`, count-up over 2600ms `easeOutQuint`, for the single number that matters (total latency, or items handled). The only motion over 700ms on the page.
- If the job has a human-approved write: `ApproveGate` before that step (`ring-float bg-white p-5`, `⛋ your call`, 900ms hold fill, `.gate-stamp` `approved` on success, confetti only there). Otherwise no stamp and no confetti anywhere.

### 3.7 Type and motion
- Onest at 430 body / 450 quiet / 480 emphasis / 340 numerals; headings by size. IBM Plex Mono 9 to 11px for every label, count, timer, path, status, operation, latency (`tabular-nums`). Departure Mono for the one numeral if the woff2 is at hand, else Plex Mono (dot ships that fallback). Two families in frame, never three.
- One curve: `--ease-signature`. `msg-in` 350 (rows), `blur-up` 420 (stage and result swaps), `stage-enter` 500 (step change), `dash-pop` 450 (badges, FAILED), `dash-pulse` 1.1s (live dot), `shimmer-sweep` 1.5s (the one live row), `dash-tick` 0.3s (count chip), `stamp-in` 500 (human approve), count-up 2600 (once). Reduced-motion guard from `tokens.css`.

## 4. Do not
- Borders as boxes; any stroke over 1px (lemma's 2px colored left rule is out; use the dot). Pure black text; pure white ground; dark mode.
- A second accent or a second gray. Red for routine state. Color as decoration. `--live` on anything but the executing step.
- Spinners, skeletons, shimmer on more than one row, a progress bar for discrete steps, "particles in void + bloom glow + orbit controls".
- Bounce or overshoot easing; hover that scales; motion over 700ms except the one count-up; confetti on system events; the `intro-wash` veil (sia, runningpod) or any decorative entrance.
- A chat transcript as the UI ("NOT a chat card"); a node graph or wires; the 3D carousel; a dashboard home; tooltips to explain a primitive.
- Gradient text, outer glows, neutral drop shadows, "3 equal cards", Inter, "Elevate/Seamless/Next-Gen" copy, round 50%/99% stats, an oversized H1.
- Mid-scale 20 to 40px padding inside components; full-bleed cards; a paragraph where a chip, a stat, or a row would do.
- Faking progress: `furthest` never moves ahead of a real success row; a skipped step is shown as skipped; a cached step is labeled; an empty result is an honest logged absence, never `[]` dressed as done.
- Hand-typed numbers anywhere on the page; every count is read from `ledger.jsonl`.
